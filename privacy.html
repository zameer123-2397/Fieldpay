Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to run your invoices: your email, your chosen payment link or bank details, and the invoices you create.

We never sell your data or share it with advertisers. Your payment details are shown only on the invoices you send to your own customers.

You can delete all your data at any time from Settings — this permanently removes your account and every invoice tied to it.

We use a single cookie to recognize your device. No tracking, no third-party analytics.
